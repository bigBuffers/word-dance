## 1. ECDICT (MIT) — every dictionary field

**ECDICT — Free English to Chinese Dictionary Database** by skywind3000, https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT — MIT License
(`data/licenses/ECDICT-LICENSE`). Release used: 1.0.28 (`ecdict-sqlite-28.zip` → `stardict.db`, the file `deploy/fetch_ecdict.sh` pins).
Fields taken: word, phonetic, definition, translation, pos, collins, oxford, tag (→ `exam_tags`, CET-4/CET-6 flags of new rows), bnc, frq, exchange, id (→ `ecdict_id`).
Phonetics are normalised (`ә→ə`, `:→ː`); a new row whose first translation line starts with `[网络] ` has that prefix removed
(`stats.network_translations` = 10). Rows already present in the previous seed keep their dictionary fields byte for byte.

## 4. Hand-written glosses — `data/lists/overrides.tsv`

28 rows (at most 30; 28 applied in this build): 24 `translation`, 3 `word`, 1 `drop`. A `translation` override replaces the whole ECDICT translation of that lemma (wrong sense: `bund`, `mum`, `p.e.`, abbreviations such as `ai`, `bce`, `un`); `word` fixes the display; `drop` keeps a duplicate out of the seed. These glosses are written for this repository and are MIT-licensed text of word-Dance, not ECDICT or official-list content.

Stage codes (`backend/wd/schemas.py`, the only place the enum lives): `xs` = 小学 (index 1) · `zk` = 初中 (index 2) · `gk` = 高中 (index 3) · `cet4` = 四级 (index 4) · `cet6` = 六级 (index 5). A record's `stages[]` is the union of its official lists
(cumulative on the national chain by construction: xs ⊆ zk; zk ⊄ gk by design) and its CET membership; `stage_min` = index of the first code,
NULL when the list is empty (课标外 — kept in the dictionary, shown only in 全部 and search). `exam_tags` keeps ECDICT's other tags (ky 考研,
toefl, ielts, gre). `exchange` codes (ECDICT): p=过去式, d=过去分词, i=现在分词, 3=第三人称单数, r=比较级, t=最高级, s=复数, 0=原型, 1=原型的变换形式.
