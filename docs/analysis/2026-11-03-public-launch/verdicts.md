## V3-china-appstore-icp-testflight — PARTIALLY_CONFIRMED (high)

CORRECTED CLAIM:
App Store Connect has an "Availability in China mainland" section (App Information tab) where Apple says China's MIIT "requires that some apps possess a valid Internet Content Provider (ICP) Filing Number" and asks developers to "if applicable, provide the required ICP filing information"; two app statuses exist, "ICP Filing Number Missing" and "ICP Filing Number Invalid", both defined as "Your app isn't available in China mainland because ..." — i.e. Apple's enforcement mechanism is storefront-availability gating of China mainland, not a review rejection or an update block. Apple added the requirement on Friday 29 Sept 2023 (Reuters: Apple "began requesting app-makers to follow the requirement last Friday, according to its website for developers"; RFA quoted the 29 Sept App Store Connect text) and it applied to new submissions first. The "1 April 2024" date is MIIT's deadline (notice 工信部信管〔2023〕105号, dated 2023-07-21, published 2023-08-04: 存量APP备案阶段 2023-09 to 2024-03, 监督检查阶段 2024-04 to 2024-06; provincial 通信管理局 notices of 4 Mar 2024 threatened 下架、关停、限制更新), not an Apple-published rule: the sentence "Starting from April 1, 2024, apps without ICP Filing Number will be unable to publish updates and face mandatory removal" originates from an appfilingchina.com blog (26 Feb 2024) repeated by a non-Apple forum user, and Apple's developer news feed (Nov 2024–Nov 2026) and help pages contain no such statement. Contemporaneous testing (游戏陀螺/七麦, 3 Apr 2024) found that mainland developers submitting NEW apps saw "必须提供 ICP 备案号才能上架", that updates of already-listed apps were not forced to verify an ICP number, and that "国际开发者即公司营业主体和服务器设在海外，则没有要求校验 ICP 备案号"; mainland developers in Jan 2025 report old apps can still submit updates but are auto-delisted from the China mainland region when no 备案号 is on file. For overseas-registered developer accounts the requirement is in practice not enforced: AppInChina (updated 20 Oct 2026) states "As of November 2025, this requirement only applies to Android app stores, as publishing in the Mainland China region from a Global Apple App Store developer account is still possible" and (28 Jul 2026) that such accounts "are prompted to provide an ICP Filing, but it is still possible to publish the app without providing one"; on 2026-11-03 the China storefront still lists Things 3, Tampermonkey (an individual German developer), Halide, Carrot Weather, Bear and Day One with updates dated Oct/Nov 2026.
