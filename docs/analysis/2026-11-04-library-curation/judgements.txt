  - Per-card AI labelling (标识办法 Art. 4, PUBLIC_LAUNCH B3, ≈0.5 day) and the AI marker in e-mail/webhook push text are deferred to 'first month' or 'later' by all three, although every example shown at launch is AI-generated and the item is cheaper than almost anything else on the lists; D3 is the only one to mention push texts at all.
  - GET /api/lookup runs a synchronous read of the 851 MB stardict.db per keystroke with only a 300 ms client debounce and no server-side quota (user-add-lookup finding 18; ecdict.py opens the file per call). All three make ECDICT the load-bearing contributor path on a public box without addressing its latency, IO, or a rate limit, and none measures the import-time risk of ECDICT's junk/[网络]/misspelt entries becoming public rows at 200 per user per day and appearing in everyone's 词库 search (stage=all).
